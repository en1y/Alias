# Alias - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Alias does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

- The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting.
- The app does not request internet access or any other permissions.
- Your settings (teams, language, theme, game options) are saved only on your device.
  If Android backup is turned on, Android may include them in your own Google account
  backup. That backup is handled by Google, and I, the developer, can't see it.

If this changes, this page will receive an update right on time.

If you have any questions, you can contact the developer through Google Play or through the developer's GitHub page: https://github.com/en1y
